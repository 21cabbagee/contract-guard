# ContractGuard: Automated Contract Redlining & Toxic Clause Guard for Freelancers

> **Instant contract scanner detecting unlimited liability, predatory IP assignments, and Net-90 clauses with 1-click counter-proposal redlines.**

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Thesis
- **Product Name:** ContractGuard
- **One-Liner:** Automated contract redlining and toxic clause guard for freelancers, consultants, and boutique agencies, generating bank-grade counter-proposals in under 30 seconds.
- **Problem Thesis:** Solo software engineers, creative directors, and boutique agencies lose thousands to indemnification traps, unlimited liability, and net-90 payment terms because hiring a contract attorney costs $500–$1,000/hr. ContractGuard scans contracts client-side, detects predatory terms, and generates copy-ready counter-clauses proven to pass enterprise procurement.
- **Bottoms-Up TAM:** 3,500,000 professional freelancers/boutique studios × $1,188/yr ($99/mo) = **$4.17 Billion TAM**.

---

## 2. In-Scope Core Features (Black Car 12-Hour MVP Scope)
1. **Interactive Redline Studio:** Real-time text & MSA document analysis flagging toxic traps.
2. **Indemnification Guard:** Replaces unlimited personal liability with a 3-month fee cap.
3. **Paid-In-Full IP Protection:** Ensures intellectual property title transfers strictly upon final invoice clearance.
4. **Payment Terms Enforcer:** Converts Net-90 and pay-when-paid into Net-15 with statutory monthly late interest.
5. **Non-Compete Scope Shrinker:** Severs overbroad 24-month industry non-competes down to standard non-solicitation.
